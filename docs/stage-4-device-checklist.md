# Stage 4: research, laws and revolts — what to try

- [ ] The tab bar has six tabs (Build, Army, Trade, Diplomacy, Research, Nation) and all fit on the iPhone in landscape, clear of the notch.
- [ ] **Research** tab as Britain: "Nothing is being researched" explains that points still bank up. Pick **Power Looms** → a progress bar with "about N months".
- [ ] Tap **Queue** on another tech → it appears under "Next up"; ✕ removes it. Switch trees with Production / Military / Society.
- [ ] A locked tech says what it needs ("Needs Steam Engine"). Try building a **Railway** as a country without the Railways tech: the Build tab says "Needs research".
- [ ] At speed 3, a tech finishes → toast "Researched …".
- [ ] **Nation** tab → **Laws**: tap an area (e.g. Education) to open it. Each option shows its effects, who likes it, the support ("Some support", "Opposed") and the chance per month. A law nobody backs has a greyed **Enact** with the reason.
- [ ] Enact a supported law → "Passing: …" with the month count, and a **Cancel** button. Months later: "… is now law" (or it fails with the reason).
- [ ] **Interest groups**: clout bars and a mood chip (Loyal / Content / Unhappy / Angry). An angry group shows its radicalism and what it wants; near 100% with enough clout: "close to revolt!".
- [ ] Revolt (hard to trigger on purpose: enact laws your workers or intellectuals hate, e.g. Serfdom and No Schooling, then wait at speed 3): a red toast, a new rebel country appears on the map at war with you. Beat them and their land comes back; give them their law in a peace deal and they go home.
- [ ] Save and load during a revolt or while a law is being passed: everything continues where it left off.
- [ ] Old saves from before this update are refused with a clear message (the save format changed).
