# Stage 5: power blocs and companies — what to try

- [ ] As **Britain**, Diplomacy tab (iPhone) or **Bloc** tab (iPad): "Found a power bloc" shows the four types; picking one shows its fixed principle; you can pick two more (a third is greyed out). Found it: the card shows your principles with your (leader) effects.
- [ ] Open **Portugal** in the Diplomacy list → **Invite to our bloc**: a toast says they joined or turned you down. Members show in your bloc card; **Expel** removes one.
- [ ] As a small country (e.g. Denmark), the bloc section explains only major powers can found blocs. When an AI bloc invites you, a toast offers **Join / Refuse** (at most twice a year per bloc).
- [ ] Start a diplomatic play while leading a bloc: "Join our power bloc" is one of the demands.
- [ ] Lose your bloc leader (or leave): the buffs disappear at once; a toast says who leads now.
- [ ] **Nation** tab → **Companies**: "Found a company" lists generic and your historical companies with costs; found one; its bonus (+X% output) grows as you build more of its buildings. A company you can't found says why ("Build more industry to run another company").
- [ ] Grant a **Monopoly**: more output, and in Interest groups the industrialists get happier and workers less so. The investment charter is greyed out without puppets.
- [ ] Save and load with a bloc and companies: everything continues.
- [ ] Old saves are refused with a clear message (the save format changed).
- [ ] iPad: seven tabs fit at the bottom (Bloc between Diplomacy and Research), in landscape and split view.
