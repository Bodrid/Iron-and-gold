# Stage 2: trade and navies — what to try

Same setup as `docs/stage-1-device-checklist.md` (the site updates itself; if it doesn't, close the app and open it again from the Home Screen — updates install when you're on the main menu).

- [ ] Play as the **United Kingdom**, speed 3: small light dots (trade ships) sail between ports within a minute or two of game time.
- [ ] **Trade** tab (5th tab): shows routes used / total, trade income, the Automatic trade switch, and lets you cancel a route. All five tabs fit along the bottom on the iPhone.
- [ ] Select one of your states, **Build → Shipyard**. When it's done, open **Army → Navy**, build a warship.
- [ ] Tap the sea: an **Open sea** card appears. In **Army → Navy**, "Patrol here" sends the warship; it sails there (triangle in your colour).
- [ ] Declare war on a coastal neighbour, send a warship to **Raid here** on their trade lane: you get "Your navy captured … trade ship" and money.
- [ ] Tap an unclaimed coast across the sea (for example as Portugal, Africa's coast) → **Send boat**: a square sails over and your troops land and spread.
- [ ] Speed 3 for ten minutes with lots of ships about: still smooth, no heat.
