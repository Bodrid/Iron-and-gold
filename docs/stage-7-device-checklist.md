# Stage 7: AI, balance and polish — what to try

- [ ] Play 1836 as a small country on **Easy**: neighbours rarely make demands of you (at most once every few years).
- [ ] Play the same country on **Hard**: AIs make demands when you are already at war or short of troops.
- [ ] **Grand Master** shows its bonuses on the difficulty screen and plays noticeably sharper.
- [ ] Over a long game (speed 3), living standards climb from about 8–12 toward 20+, and populations grow at a believable pace (no country triples in 50 years).
- [ ] As Prussia, the German states are protected by Austria and Prussia's guarantees (the German Confederation); the unification events can still bring them together.
- [ ] Every toast, including sticky ones, has a ✕.
- [ ] Diplomacy → a country: "Improve relations" shows its monthly cost.
- [ ] Trade tab: each route shows what it earns per month.
- [ ] As a bloc leader, "Change principles" is available once a year.
- [ ] On a desktop, Cmd/Ctrl+R reloads the page instead of opening the Research tab.
