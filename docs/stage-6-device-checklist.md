# Stage 6: events, nations and 1776 — what to try

- [ ] **New game** shows two start dates, 1836 and 1776. Picking 1776 changes the country list (Poland-Lithuania, Venice, the Maratha Confederacy, no United States).
- [ ] Start as **Great Britain in 1776** at speed 3: within a couple of months the card "The Declaration of Independence" appears (toast → Open). Each choice lists its effects in plain words. Choosing "Crush the rebellion" puts the United States on the map at war with you.
- [ ] Event cards: "Later" closes the card; it waits in Nation tab → "Waiting for you" with the days left; after 30 days the first option is taken automatically. Answered events appear under "Recent events".
- [ ] Never more than two cards wait at once; more come after you answer.
- [ ] As **Sardinia-Piedmont in 1836**, Nation tab → Decisions shows "Proclaim Italy" with "you own X of its 15 states". Over the years the Risorgimento and Garibaldi events offer ways to get there.
- [ ] As **Prussia in 1836**, watch the Zollverein, Schleswig-Holstein, North German Confederation and German Empire events. Proclaiming Germany changes your name and colour on the map and keeps your treaties and bloc.
- [ ] As **Japan**, the Black Ships arrive in the 1850s; opening the ports changes your trade law and gives research.
- [ ] Independence wars (Bulgaria, the Confederacy, the United States): the new country fights; if it wins it stays on the map, if it loses its land returns.
- [ ] Save and load with an event card waiting: it is still there.
- [ ] Old saves are refused with a clear message (the save format changed).
