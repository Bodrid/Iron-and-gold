# Stage 3: diplomacy — what to try

- [ ] **Diplomacy** tab: infamy and diplomats at the top; every country with a relation chip (Hostile → Friendly).
- [ ] Tap a country → its panel: Improve relations uses a diplomat; treaty buttons say why they're unavailable (e.g. "Needs Cordial relations").
- [ ] As **Prussia**, open **Denmark** → Start a diplomatic play → Take Schleswig-Holstein → Start. The play shows a countdown and both sides' strength; at speed 3, within ~10 seconds Denmark either gives in (the state turns grey) or war begins.
- [ ] Tap an enemy state on the map → **Demand this state** starts a play for it directly.
- [ ] In a war, **Diplomacy → Make peace…**: your war goals are pre-picked; extra states cost more points; the button greys out when you ask for too much. "White peace" ends it with no changes.
- [ ] When an AI makes demands of you, a red toast appears; **Give in** in the Diplomacy tab, or wait and fight.
- [ ] Grab a lot of land quickly: infamy rises; above 50 a coalition may form against you (toast).
